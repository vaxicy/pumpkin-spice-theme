Pumpkin Spice is a cozy autumn Chrome theme built on a warm earth palette: a deep espresso frame, a cinnamon-brown toolbar, and a soft cream New Tab Page warmed by oat and pumpkin accents. Espresso ink keeps every tab, bookmark, and icon crisp against the pale surfaces, while links take on a burnt-pumpkin tone. A single-row shortcut layout and a color-matched Google logo keep the page clean and uncluttered. Solid colors only, no wallpaper — warm, calm, and easy on the eyes.
