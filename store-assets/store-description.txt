Pumpkin Spice is a cozy autumn Chrome theme built on a warm earth palette: a deep espresso frame, a cinnamon-brown toolbar, and a soft cream New Tab Page warmed by oat and pumpkin accents. Cream ink keeps every tab, bookmark and toolbar icon crisp against the dark chrome, while espresso text and burnt-pumpkin links keep the pale page easy to read. A single-row shortcut layout and a color-matched Google logo keep the page clean and uncluttered. Solid colors only, no wallpaper — warm, calm, and easy on the eyes.
